Methodology and data quality

    Total volume is DefiLlama’s reported DEX volume for a closed UTC day. The market-cap split is estimated from sampled pools. Unknown market caps and unsampled volume are currently assigned to the below-$100M bucket; where sampled volume exceeds the reference total, the split is scaled proportionally. Coverage is not an accuracy score.

    Classification uses the pair’s non-quote token. Pairs with two quote assets count as above $100M. Historical volume is grouped using the market-cap snapshot shown above, rather than each trading day’s market cap.

    30-day change compares the trailing 30 non-provisional observations with the 30 before. Faded bars mark recent provisional days, which are excluded from the summary. The chart range changes the plotted history; the summary always covers 30 days.
