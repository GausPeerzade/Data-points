Methodology and data quality

    Total per day is DefiLlama's chain DEX volume for the closed UTC day. Above / below $100M is assigned per trading pair by the market cap of the pair's non-quote token (stablecoin and native-asset pairs count as above); tokens with no verifiable market cap count as below and are reported as "unknown". The 30-day change compares the trailing 30 complete days with the 30 before. Faded bars are provisional: DefiLlama had not published every venue for that day at refresh time; they are excluded from the KPIs and re-pulled on the next run. Full details in ARCHITECTURE.md.
